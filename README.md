# Horsing Around: Over the Shoulder

A Red Dead Redemption 2 style over-the-shoulder camera for Minecraft. Third person (back) sits just off your
shoulder and looks exactly where you look; the centre crosshair stays true, so blocks, mobs, arrows, tridents and thrown
items go where it points. Aiming a bow, crossbow or trident pulls the camera in close.

Works on its own. With [Horsing Around](https://github.com/GabePurc/horsing-around) installed it also takes over the
riding camera: it pulls back as the horse speeds up and rides with the saddle.

## Controls

- **O**: swap shoulders.
- An unbound key to turn the camera on or off, and one to open its settings (Controls, under "Over the Shoulder").
- Everything else is vanilla: F5 cycles first person, over the shoulder, and the front view.

## Settings

Distance, shoulder offset and height on foot, aiming and riding; through [Mod Menu](https://modrinth.com/mod/modmenu)
or the settings key. Saved in `config/horsingaround_shoulder.json`.

## Compatibility

Client only: works on any server. If Shoulder Surfing Reloaded is installed, that mod's camera wins while it is on
(they do the same job).

## Requirements

Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, and Fabric API. Mod Menu and Horsing Around are optional.
