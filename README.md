# Horsing Around: Over the Shoulder

> Third person, the way it should have been all along.

[Buy me a coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/blintzbug) ☕

Vanilla third person parks the camera dead centre behind your head, so you spend half your time staring at the back of
your own skull and the other half guessing where your arrows are going to land. I wanted something better: the camera
tucked just off your shoulder, the world wide open in front of you, and a crosshair you can actually trust.

So that's what this is.

## What you get

**A camera over your shoulder.** It sits just off your right shoulder and looks exactly where you look, so turning feels
natural and nothing swims around. Prefer the left? Press **O** to swap sides.

**A crosshair that tells the truth.** Blocks, mobs, arrows, tridents, snowballs: everything goes where the centre
crosshair points, not where the back of your head happens to be facing. Mine, fight and shoot like you would in first
person, while seeing your whole character.

**It leans in when you aim.** Draw a bow, load a crossbow or ready a trident and the camera pulls in close over your
shoulder for the shot, then eases back out when you're done.

**Walls don't trap it.** Back up against a wall or squeeze through a tunnel and the camera slides in instead of
clipping through the scenery.

**It rides beautifully.** Install it with [Horsing Around](https://github.com/GabePurc/horsing-around) and it takes over
the riding camera too: it pulls back as your horse picks up speed and moves with the saddle, so a full gallop across the
plains looks like a movie.

## Controls

- **O**: swap shoulders.
- **F5**: cycles first person, over the shoulder and the front view, just like vanilla.
- Two keys you can bind yourself (under "Over the Shoulder" in Controls): turn the camera on or off, and open its
  settings.

## Make it yours

Distance, shoulder offset and height for walking around, for aiming, and for riding, all adjustable in
[Mod Menu](https://modrinth.com/mod/modmenu) or with the settings key. It all saves to
`config/horsingaround_shoulder.json`.

## Works everywhere

It's entirely client-side: put it in your own game and it works in singleplayer, on your friend's server, on a big public
server, anywhere. Nobody else needs to install a thing.

One heads-up: Shoulder Surfing Reloaded does the same job, so if you have both installed, Shoulder Surfing takes the
camera whenever it's on. Pick whichever one you like best.
